# go.trendit.in short links

Cloudflare Worker that powers the free URL shortener and click counter on
VL Tech Official. Designed and developed by Vijaya Kumar L (RisewithVJ).

Links are checked for safety before they are created, visits are rate limited,
and every short link opens through the blog with a short wait.
